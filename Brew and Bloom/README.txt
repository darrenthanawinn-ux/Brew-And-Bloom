Brew & Bloom — A Cozy Tea House
================================

How to play:
1. Extract this zip anywhere on your computer.
2. Double-click "index.html" to open it in your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari all work).
3. No internet connection or installation needed — everything runs locally in the page.

Gameplay:
- A forest critter arrives at the counter with a tea request (see the speech bubble and flavor tags).
- Drag (or tap) tea leaves, flowers, and spices from the tray into the teapot.
- Pour hot water, adjust the temperature slider, set a steep time, then press Steep.
- Pour the finished tea into the mug, add an optional garnish, then press "Serve the tea."
- Earn Cozy Coins and hearts based on how well your tea matches the request.
- Spend coins in the Shop (top right) on decor, music, and rare seeds.
- Plant rare seeds in the Windowsill Garden and tend them to grow rare ingredients.
- Adjust Master/Music/SFX volume, ambience, and day/night warmth in Settings (gear icon).

Your progress (coins, unlocks, garden, settings) is saved automatically to your browser's
local storage, so it will still be there next time you open index.html in the same browser.
